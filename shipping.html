Shipping Policy

Order Processing

All orders are processed and dispatched within 24–48 business hours (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays).

Shipping Charges

Complimentary shipping across India on orders of ₹1,999 and above. A flat ₹99 shipping fee applies on orders below ₹1,999.

Delivery Timeline

Metro cities typically receive deliveries within 3–5 business days after dispatch. Other serviceable pincodes across India take 5–7 business days.

Tracking

Once shipped, your tracking details are available on the Order Tracking page.

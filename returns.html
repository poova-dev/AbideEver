Returns & Exchanges

7-Day Return & Exchange Window

You may request an exchange or return within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags attached.

How to Initiate

Write to care@abideever.com or use our Contact form with your Order ID and reason for return/exchange.

Refunds

Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after quality inspection.
